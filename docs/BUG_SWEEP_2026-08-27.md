## A. Fix first

### A1. A questionnaire she has never taken is offered to her as a "Reassessment due", and opens without a coach ever assigning it

**Screen:** `/questionnaires` (Premium section) and `/assessments/nutrition-lifestyle`, and the direct URL `/assessments/nutrition-lifestyle/take`.

**What she sees:** a card headed *Nutrition & Lifestyle Questionnaire*, badged
**Reassessment due**, with a dark green primary button that says **Start
Reassessment**. Tapping it opens *Question 1 of 91*. She has never completed this
questionnaire, no coach has assigned it, and it is marked `requiresAssignment:
true` and membership-tier in the registry. Every other coach-assign-only
questionnaire on the same screen (Four Doctors, Health Check-In, Primal Pattern,
Whole-Body Check-In) correctly refuses the same direct URL with *"Not assigned
yet. Your coach will assign this when the time is right."*

**Reproduce:**
1. Sign in as the test member and open `/questionnaires`.
2. Scroll to **PREMIUM**.
3. Tap **Start Reassessment** (or type `/assessments/nutrition-lifestyle/take`).

**Why:** [status.ts:107-114](apps/consumer-web-app/lib/assessment-registry/status.ts#L107-L114)
lets a pending `reassessment_schedules` row satisfy the assignment gate. Its own
comment says a pending schedule *"only ever exists for an assessment the member
already has real history with."* On production that premise is false.
[reassessment-intelligence/service.ts:56-85](apps/consumer-web-app/lib/reassessment-intelligence/service.ts#L56-L85)
maps a worsening finding in a *domain* onto an assessment key and schedules a
"reassessment" without ever checking whether that assessment was assessed.

**Measured on production:** 6 pending `reassessment_schedules` rows exist. **4 of
them are for an assessment the member has never completed**, across three
accounts, one of which is a real (non-test) tester:

| member | assessment scheduled | ever completed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `3e7af809` (real tester) | four-doctors | no |
| `ab25b880` (test member) | chek-hlc1-nutrition-lifestyle | no |
| `72bd763f` | four-doctors | no |
| `72bd763f` | **body-assessment** | no |

The last row means the camera Body Assessment is unlocked for an account that
was never assigned it.

**Affects:** any member the daily coaching scan has ever raised a worsening
nutrition, stress, sleep, posture, movement or breathing finding for. Three
accounts today, one of them a real tester.

**Screenshot:** `scripts/.sweep/shots/questionnaires.png`, `assessments_nutrition_lifestyle_take.png`

**Fix size:** small. Two independent guards, either of which closes it: make
`calculateLockReason` honour a pending schedule only when `hasEverCompleted(facts)`,
and make `evaluateReassessmentTriggers` refuse to schedule a reassessment of
something never assessed.

### A2. Opening a take URL still creates a draft on render, in the two flows migration 186 did not cover

**Screen:** `/assessments/<slug>/take` (generic engine: Nutrition & Lifestyle, Four Doctors, Health Check-In) and `/assessments/primal-pattern-diet-type/take`.

**What happens:** the page component calls
[getMyTakeAssessmentState](apps/consumer-web-app/app/actions/assessments.ts#L176-L206)
during render, which calls `getOrCreateInProgressAssessment`. Its own comment in
[take/page.tsx:22-25](apps/consumer-web-app/app/assessments/[questionnaireId]/take/page.tsx#L22-L25)
says out loud that it *"would otherwise create a fresh in-progress draft row on
its very first call"*. This is the exact shape
`startOrResumeSession` was fixed for on 2026-08-27 for Core Values Snapshot, Life
Signal Check, Readiness Pulse and WBSA. It was never applied here. There is no
`already_completed` short-circuit and no `?retake=1` gate: a completed
questionnaire re-opened from the browser Back button, a refresh, or a bookmark
silently starts a blank retake.

**Proven live, by accident, during this very sweep.** A read-only page load of
`/assessments/nutrition-lifestyle/take` created `wellness_assessments` row
`d83a451a-a9cf-4c0b-a359-7880943436a5` (`in_progress`, zero answers) on the test
member's account. See "State this sweep left on production" at the bottom.

**Reproduce:**
1. Open `/assessments/nutrition-lifestyle/take`.
2. Press the browser Back button, then Forward.
3. Query `wellness_assessments` for that member: a draft exists that no button created.

**Affects:** every member with access to a generic-engine questionnaire or Primal
Pattern. Once the draft exists, the overview screen changes its call to action to
*"Resume assessment"* and reports *"0 of 91 questions completed"*.

**Fix size:** medium. The same `already_completed` / `?retake=1` shape the four
runtime flows now use, applied to `getMyTakeAssessmentState` and
`getMyPrimalPatternTakeState`.

**Screen:** `/dashboard` (and it disagrees with `/today`, `/progress`, `/case` and the coach's view).

**What she sees, all on Home, scrolling once:**

| where | sentence |
| --- | --- |
| hero, under the score | "You have **3 logged days** so far." |
| Root's Daily Brief, Root Score row | "You have **3 logged days** so far." |
| Root's Daily Brief, Root's own line | "You've logged **4 check-ins** with me so far, and I still have every one of them." |

And elsewhere: `/today` → "**4** Check-ins logged". `/progress` History → **4**

counts all time. The Root Score domains count over their own 30-day and 24-day
windows. The member's real dates are Aug 17, Aug 16, Aug 14 and Aug 4, so 3 is
correct for 21 days and 4 is correct for all time.

**Reproduce:**
1. Open Home as a member whose oldest check-in is more than 21 days ago.
2. Read the sentence under the Root Score.
3. Scroll to Root's Daily Brief and read the quoted line.

**Affects:** any member with a check-in older than 21 days and a gap since. The
maths is right everywhere; the words are wrong in at least one place.

**Screenshot:** `scripts/.sweep/shots/fp_dashboard.png`, `chart__root_score_1Week.png`

**Fix size:** small. Either name the window in `dataFloorStatement` ("in the last
three weeks") or have both sentences read the same count.

## B. Fix in the same build

### B1. The Priority Card pop-up starves everything below it in the chain, including the Weekly Root Review

**Screen:** the pop-up that opens on Home.

[rootPopupMessages.ts:353](apps/consumer-web-app/app/actions/rootPopupMessages.ts#L353)
(the re-entry takeover) and
[rootPopupMessages.ts:620](apps/consumer-web-app/app/actions/rootPopupMessages.ts#L620)
(the ordinary daily card) both `return` a `priority_card` message **without
checking whether it is still due, and without falling through to the next
candidate.** `getMyRootPopupMessageAction` then finds the dismissal row and turns
the whole call into `null`.

This is the precise failure the file's own header describes twice and the shared
helper's doc comment names in so many words:

> "a caller that returns the first candidate unconditionally, without checking
> due-ness itself and falling through to the next, reintroduces that exact bug for
> whichever message type skips it"
> — [pickFirstDueOneTimeMessage](apps/consumer-web-app/lib/root-popup-messages/data.ts#L192-L211)

Every other branch in the chain (hydration, cvs/lsc/rpl day3, day7, offers, reset
plan, weekly review, coach assignments) does the guarded fall-through. The two
priority-card branches do not.

**Live on production right now, for the test member:**

- `member_root_popup_dismissals` holds `priority_card:2026-08-27`, status `ignored`.
- `member_daily_priorities` for 2026-08-27 is rule `re_entry`, status `active`.
- `member_weekly_reviews` holds `week_start = 2026-08-24`, `viewed_at = null`, `acknowledged_at = null`, and there is **no** `weekly_review:2026-08-24` dismissal row, so that review is genuinely due.
- Loading `/dashboard` shows **no pop-up at all**. The re-entry branch returns first, the outer check kills it, and the review she has never seen cannot be delivered today.

Because the card is auto-dismissed on mount, this fires on the very first Home
open of each day. The re-entry variant is the worse half: it sits *above* the
hydration question, every day-3/day-7 follow-up and the weekly review, and
re-entry is exactly the state a returning member is in when those are waiting.

**Reproduce:**
1. Open Home once, so the day's priority pop-up is shown and auto-dismissed.
2. Reload Home.
3. No pop-up appears, and nothing lower in the chain can appear for the rest of the day.

**Affects:** every member, every day the priority card is still active. The ordinary
branch starves the free-arc invitation; the re-entry branch starves everything
below it.

**Fix size:** small. Wrap both in the same due-check-then-fall-through the other
branches already use.

### B3. `/admin/access` throws React hydration errors #418, #423 and #425 on every load

**Screen:** `/admin/access`.

Captured live: three uncaught page errors on load, minified React #418, #423 and
#425 — the exact three codes named in
[lib/time/displayDate.ts](apps/consumer-web-app/lib/time/displayDate.ts)'s own
header as the hydration-mismatch family.

**Why:** [MemberAccessPanel.tsx:42](apps/consumer-web-app/app/admin/access/MemberAccessPanel.tsx#L42)
is a client component formatting a stored timestamp with
`date.toLocaleDateString(undefined, …)` — no locale, no `timeZone`. Vercel renders
it in UTC with the server's ICU default; the browser renders it in the
administrator's own zone and locale. Trial timestamps near a day boundary
therefore produce different text on the two passes.

The shared fix already exists and is not used here: `formatDisplayDate`, which
pins `timeZone: 'UTC'` and returns `'date not available'` rather than an
`Invalid Date`.

**Reproduce:**
1. Sign in as an administrator, open dev tools, load `/admin/access`.
2. Read the console.

**Affects:** administrators. Three trial/assignment dates per member card, 12
members.
**Fix size:** small. Route the three `formatDate` calls through `formatDisplayDate`.

### B4. A take-page render writes a router-decision row every single time

[getMyTakeAssessmentState](apps/consumer-web-app/app/actions/assessments.ts#L195-L203)
calls `decideNextAction` and `recordRouterDecision` on **every** render of the
take page, not only when a new attempt is started.
`investigation_router_decisions` is append-only with no uniqueness, so a
refresh, a Back-then-Forward, or a Server Action revalidation each adds a row.

That table exists to keep the Root Model honest about
chosen-versus-recommended, so inflating it with page views is a quiet
falsification of the one log built to catch that. (Confirmed by this sweep: the
test member had 0 rows before the walk and 1 after a single page load.)

**Affects:** the Root Model's own honesty check. No member sees it.
**Fix size:** small. Move the call to the branch that genuinely starts an attempt.

### B5. The coaching insight counts snapshots the member never produced, and calls five of them "your last 5"

**Screen:** `/progress`, COACHING INSIGHTS panel.

**What she reads:** *"4 of your last 5 progress snapshots showed improving overall
momentum."* Her Root Score, on the same page, is **27 and 1 point down**, and her
last check-in was ten days earlier.

Both halves are defensible on their own and wrong together:

- The engine is honest by its own rule.
  [progressSource.ts](apps/consumer-web-app/lib/coaching-insights/sources/progressSource.ts)
  emits nothing for an `insufficient_data` snapshot, so "the last 5" means the
  last five snapshots that carried a real momentum state: Aug 15, 16, 17, 21, 23,
  four of them positive. The maths is correct.
- The **words** are not. Her last five *snapshots* are Aug 19, 20, 21, 23 and 27,
  of which three are `insufficient_data` and two are improving. She cannot
  reconcile the sentence with anything she can see, and the stored explanation
  says "Aug 15–Aug 23", a range that actually holds eight snapshots, not five.
- Underneath both: `root_score_snapshots` rows exist for Aug 18 through Aug 27,
  every one of them created by a page render on a day she logged nothing. The
  insight is reading the app's own arithmetic about her silence and reporting it
  to her as momentum.

**Reproduce:**
1. Stop checking in for a week, then open `/progress`.
2. Read the COACHING INSIGHTS headline.
3. Compare with the Root Score above it.

**Affects:** any member with a gap. **Fix size:** medium. Name what is actually
being counted ("4 of your last 5 days with a reading"), and decide whether a
snapshot with no new member input should count as evidence at all.

## Your call

Not ranked, because each is defensible either way.

**Y1. A coach-assign-only assessment becomes permanently self-serve after one
completion.** [checkAssessmentAccess](apps/consumer-web-app/lib/assessment-registry/access.ts#L50)
lets anyone through whose `completionStatus !== 'not_started'`. The rule it
implements is "tier gating never hides a member's own pre-existing progress",
which is right for *reading* results. But it also lets her *start a new one*: the
test member's `/assessment/new?type=static_posture` opened the camera Body
Assessment wizard, unassigned, because she finished one in August.
*Keep it:* a member can retake her own history whenever she likes. *Change it:*
reading past results stays open, starting a new attempt needs a fresh assignment.

**Y2. Visiting Movement, Root Map, Recommendations or Root Score fixes the day's
priority.** `claimDailyPriority` is deliberately an insert-if-absent with a real
unique index, and it is reached from six render paths through `TodaysFocusLine`.
So the day's one priority is decided by whichever screen she happens to open
first, before Root has necessarily seen her check-in.
*Keep it:* the decision is fixed for the day and can never be silently rewritten.
*Change it:* claim it only from the two screens that actually show the card.

**Y3. The standing live test member is not flagged as a test account.**
`8weeks2fab@gmail.com` (`ab25b880`) has `profiles.is_test = false`, so every
verification run and every sweep like this one lands in the funnel, the
engagement report, the drop-off numbers and the coach's caseload as a real
member. *Keep it:* she exercises the exact code path a real member does, including
the analytics writes. *Change it:* set the flag and accept that the analytics
paths are then no longer exercised end to end by any live run.

**Class 8, error and empty states — clean.** Across 45 member screens: zero
